# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Global English readers looking for help with automation, hosting and everyday
technology problems. The owner confirmed this audience in the current session.
Readers may be following a guide while working on the affected device or service.

## Product Purpose

A2Z Skills publishes practical troubleshooting and setup guides, alongside
sourced explanations. The owner wants organic discovery and useful Google
Search clicks. The stated target is 10,000 monthly clicks; no private traffic
baseline has been supplied, and the target is not a forecast.

## Positioning

Tested procedures show the versions, date, named tester and the errors found
during testing. Explainers link to documentation or research. These two kinds
of evidence must stay distinct.

## Operating Context

Readers arrive through search, browse topic collections, search articles,
follow section links and read a procedure. They can adjust text size, listen
using browser speech and use auto-scroll. Article URLs can be shared directly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing stack is Astro with static assets on Cloudflare Workers. Search
uses Pagefind. Hosting must remain on the existing free tier. The repository's
publishing, performance and accessibility checks apply. Article URLs, metadata,
canonical rules and crawlable content must survive a visual redesign.

A person tests every published procedure. Missing evidence, versions, prices,
author identities or results cannot be invented. Email delivery is inactive;
the signup currently records notification-list interest.

## Brand Commitments

The name is A2Z Skills, published by AntByte Labs, at a2zskillsewa.com.
The voice is plain, concise British English. The owner explicitly prefers
friendly sans-serif reading text and now asks for a clean, simple, professional
and friendly appearance. The owner has no reference websites to supply.

## Evidence on Hand

Article files, source references, author records and tested procedure metadata
are in src/content/. SEO research and a keyword queue are in docs/seo/.
Fourteen generated editorial illustrations are available, with provenance and
prompts in docs/design/. Illustrations are not test screenshots. Search Console
is verified according to the owner, but its private reports are unavailable.

## Product Principles

- Help a reader identify the relevant answer quickly.
- Keep the answer readable while they carry out a task.
- Describe only the testing and sourcing evidence that exists.
- Preserve fast, crawlable pages and useful links.
- Keep ongoing hosting within the existing free resources.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts,
browser zoom, reduced-motion support and operation without JavaScript for
article reading and normal navigation.
