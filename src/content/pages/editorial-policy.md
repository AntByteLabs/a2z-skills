This page is the standard every post on the site is held to. If a post falls
short of it, that is a bug — tell us and we will fix the post.

## Testing

How-to, fix and review posts are tested on a real machine or handset before
publishing. The build runs a check that refuses to publish those post types
without a recorded test: date, software versions, and device where relevant.

Explainers, comparisons and lists are not always hands-on. They never claim to
be, and they do not carry a "tested on" box.

## Updating and re-testing

Every tested post gets a re-test date, 120 days after the last test by default,
or sooner when the software ships a major version. A weekly job lists the posts
that are due and opens a ticket for each batch. When a post is re-tested its
update date changes; when only a typo is fixed, it does not.

## Corrections

Factual errors are corrected in place, with the update date changed. Where a
correction changes the advice, we say what was wrong at the top of the post. We
do not quietly delete posts that turned out to be mistaken.

## AI assistance

We use AI tools to draft and to check prose. A person tests every procedure,
edits every draft, and is named as the author. No post is published that a human
has not run through. Facts, numbers, version strings and error messages come
from the test, never from a model.

## Money

Some posts contain affiliate links, always disclosed before the first one.
Placement is never sold: a tool is recommended because it did the job in
testing, and the post says what it was worse at. We do not accept payment for
reviews, rankings or links, and sponsored content, if we ever run it, will be
labelled as such in the headline. See the
[affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
