A2Z Skills publishes tested procedures and researched explanations. The article
label and its evidence should make clear which one you are reading.

## Testing

A person must test a procedure before it is published. How-to, fix and review
posts must record the tester, date, software versions and relevant device. The
build checks that those records are present. It cannot verify that a person
performed a test; the named record and observed outputs provide that evidence.

A test applies to the versions and conditions recorded. It is not proof that
the same commands work on every later release. Source checks and copy edits do
not change the original test date.

Explainers use linked documentation or research. They do not carry a hands-on
test box unless an actual test is recorded. A hypothetical example illustrates a
concept; it is not a measured result. Relabelling an untested procedure as an
explainer does not meet the testing requirement.

## Updating and re-testing

The maintenance script identifies tested posts due for re-testing, normally
120 days after the recorded test. A major software change may warrant an earlier
review. A due date is a reminder, not evidence that a new test has happened.

Substantive factual corrections change the article's update date. A typo fix or
a review that finds no necessary changes does not make an old article newly
tested. The date in the test box remains the date of the recorded test.

## Corrections

Errors are corrected at the existing URL. When a correction changes the advice,
a visible note explains the previous claim and its limits. We do not quietly
remove a post to hide a mistake. Readers can send the affected URL, version and
error to the editor using the contact below.

## AI assistance

AI tools assist with drafting, editing and source checking. Their output can
contain mistakes. Factual explanations need supporting sources; test versions,
error messages and observed results must come from an actual test record.

A byline identifies the person accountable for a post. It does not establish
that the person tested a procedure or personally reviewed each revision. Those
claims require a separate record. Earlier wording on this page claimed a human
had reviewed every published draft; the available records did not establish
that for every explainer, so that blanket claim has been removed.

Editorial illustrations are labelled as illustrations. They are not screenshots
or evidence that a procedure was tested.

## Money

Affiliate links are disclosed before the first one. Recommendations need
supporting evidence; a placement is not a testing result. We do not accept
payment for reviews, rankings or links. Sponsored content, if introduced, must
be labelled. See the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
