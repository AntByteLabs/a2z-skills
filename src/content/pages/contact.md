There is no contact form, on purpose: email gets a real reply.

- **A step did not work** — [editor@a2zskillsewa.com](mailto:editor@a2zskillsewa.com)
- **Everything else** — [hello@a2zskillsewa.com](mailto:hello@a2zskillsewa.com)

## If you are reporting a broken step

Please include:

1. The post's address.
2. The step number you got stuck on.
3. Your versions — operating system build and the app version.
4. What the screen said, word for word, or a screenshot.

With those four things a fix usually takes one reply. Without them it takes
five.

We read everything. We answer corrections first, then questions, then
everything else.
