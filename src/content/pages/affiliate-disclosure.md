Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy something through one,
AntByte Labs may be paid a commission by the seller. You pay the same price
either way.

This disclosure is made to satisfy the US Federal Trade Commission's
endorsement guides, the UK Competition and Markets Authority's rules on paid
promotion, and the equivalent rules elsewhere: where there is money behind a
link, it is labelled before you click it, not in a footer.

## What that pays for

Commissions and ads pay for the software licences, test hardware and the time
spent reproducing problems. That is the whole business model: there is no paid
placement.

## The rules we hold ourselves to

- Affiliate links are disclosed on the post, above the first link, every time.
- A tool is recommended because it did the job in testing. If the best tool for
  a job has no affiliate programme, it still wins the recommendation.
- Every recommendation says what the tool is worse at.
- Rankings, inclusion and ordering are never for sale.
- Affiliate links are marked `rel="sponsored nofollow"` and route through
  `/go/`, so they are easy to spot and are not followed by search engines.

## How to avoid them

You can always go to the vendor directly by typing their address into your
browser. Nothing on the site is hidden behind an affiliate link.
