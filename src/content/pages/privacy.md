The short version: no advertising cookies, no account needed to read anything,
and the only personal data we hold is an email address you typed in yourself.

## Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not fingerprint
visitors or build profiles. We see aggregate page views, referrers and countries.
We cannot see individual people.

We also read Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, which report the
search queries that reach the site in aggregate.

## The newsletter

If you subscribe, we store your email address, the page you subscribed from, and
the date, in a Cloudflare D1 database on Cloudflare's network. We use it to send
the newsletter and nothing else. We do not sell it, rent it or share it.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To be removed immediately, email
[hello@a2zskillsewa.com](mailto:hello@a2zskillsewa.com) and the row is deleted.

Sign-up is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that you are not a
bot without tracking you across sites.

## Server logs

Cloudflare keeps standard request logs, which include IP addresses, for a short
period for security and abuse handling.

## Affiliate links

Clicking an affiliate link takes you to the vendor, who will set their own
cookies under their own policy. That is their processing, not ours. See the
[affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Your rights

If you are in the UK, EU or another region with data protection rights, you can
ask what we hold about you and have it deleted. Email
[hello@a2zskillsewa.com](mailto:hello@a2zskillsewa.com).
