and the only personal data we hold is an email address you typed in yourself.

## What we collect, and why

| What                                                                             | Why                                     | How long                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Your email address, the page you subscribed from, the date and your country code | To send the newsletter you asked for    | Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it |
| Aggregate page views, referrers and countries                                    | To know which guides are worth updating | Rolling, in aggregate only                   |
| Standard server request logs, including IP addresses                             | Security and abuse handling             | Short retention, set by Cloudflare           |

That is the whole list. We do not collect names, we do not ask you to register,
and we do not build profiles of readers.

## Analytics without tracking

We use **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, which is cookieless and does not
fingerprint visitors or follow anyone between sites. We see totals; we cannot see
individual people.

We also read **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster Tools**, which report
the search queries that reach the site in aggregate. Neither tells us who
searched.

## The newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address, the page you subscribed from, the date and
your two-letter country code are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database on
Cloudflare's network. We use them to send the newsletter and for nothing else. We
do not sell, rent or share the list, and we do not send anyone else's
advertising.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To be removed immediately, email
[hello@a2zskillsewa.com](mailto:hello@a2zskillsewa.com) and the row is deleted
outright, not flagged.

Sign-up is protected by **Cloudflare Turnstile**, which checks that you are not a
bot without tracking you across sites.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can email us and ask what we hold about you, ask for a
copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We do not require an account
or any proof beyond control of the address in question.

**UK, EU and Switzerland (GDPR and UK GDPR).** You have the rights of access,
rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to
withdraw consent at any time. Our lawful basis for the newsletter is your
consent; for server logs it is our legitimate interest in keeping the site up and
secure. You may complain to your national supervisory authority, or to the
Information Commissioner's Office in the UK.

**California (CCPA and CPRA).** You may request to know, delete or correct your
personal information, and you may not be discriminated against for asking. **We
do not sell or share personal information, and we have never done so**, so there
is no opt-out to exercise. We do not use personal information for cross-context
behavioural advertising.

**Elsewhere.** If your country gives you stronger rights than these, tell us
which and we will honour them.

## Children

This site is written for adults troubleshooting their own devices. We do not
knowingly collect anything from children under 16. If a child's address reached
our list, email us and it will be deleted.

## Affiliate links

Clicking an affiliate link takes you to the vendor, who sets their own cookies
under their own policy. That processing is theirs, not ours, and we receive no
personal information back — only an anonymous record that a sale happened. See
the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Security

The site is static HTML served over HTTPS. The newsletter database is reachable
only by the one function that writes to it, and exports are read-only and limited
to counts. We do not store payment details because we never take payments.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes and
the change is described here. The current version is always the one on this page.
